Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy describes how this application ("the App") handles information in connection with its use of TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube APIs.

1. Information Collected

The App accesses only the information necessary to publish content to social media accounts explicitly connected by its operator. This may include:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by each connected platform
  • Basic account identifiers (such as username and account ID) needed to direct posts to the correct account
  • Video files and associated metadata (titles, descriptions, hashtags) selected by the operator for publishing

2. How Information Is Used

Information collected is used solely to authenticate with each platform's API and to publish content on behalf of the operator's own connected accounts. No data collected by the App is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to publishing the operator's own content.

3. Data Storage

Access tokens and account credentials are stored locally within the operator's own infrastructure (including self-hosted environments) and are not transmitted to or stored by any third party beyond what is required to complete the publishing request to the respective platform's API.

4. Data Sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or share any collected information with third parties. Data is only transmitted to the specific platform (TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube) for which a post is being published, using that platform's own official API.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

The operator may revoke the App's access to any connected account at any time via that platform's account settings, which will immediately invalidate any stored tokens for that account. The operator may also delete locally stored video files, metadata, and credentials at any time.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children and is not intended for use by individuals under the minimum age required by each connected platform's own terms of service.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the operator directly through the platform on which this application is registered.
